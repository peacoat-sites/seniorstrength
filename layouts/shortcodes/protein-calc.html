Why so much protein? Research shows older adults need 25–50% more protein than younger adults to achieve the same muscle protein synthesis response — a phenomenon called "anabolic resistance." Spreading protein across 3–4 meals (rather than eating it all at once) maximizes absorption and muscle building throughout the day.
