Fall Risk Self-Assessment
Important: This assessment is for informational purposes. It does not replace evaluation by a physician or physical therapist. If you have concerns about your fall risk, consult your healthcare provider.

How to do it: Sit in a chair with your arms crossed over your chest. On “go,” rise to a full standing position, then sit back down. Count how many times you can complete this in 30 seconds, without using your arms for support.

How to do it: You need a stopwatch and a clear 10-foot path. Start seated in a chair. When ready, stand up (you may use the armrests), walk 10 feet to a mark on the floor, turn around, walk back, and sit down. Record the total time in seconds. Wear your regular footwear and use any walking aid you normally use.

Source: Podsiadlo & Richardson (1991); CDC TUG reference thresholds.
