Helen Santos has been teaching fitness classes designed for adults over 60 for more than a decade at community centers, senior facilities, and online. She has a gift for making movement accessible and encouraging for people who have not exercised in years or who are working around chronic conditions. At Senior Strength Guide, she covers group exercise formats, seated workouts, and staying active in a social context.
