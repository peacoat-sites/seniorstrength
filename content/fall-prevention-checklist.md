Falls are the leading cause of injury for adults 65 and older, and many happen at home on stairs, in the bathroom, or on the way there at night. Most of the fixes are simple: better lighting, fewer tripping hazards, grab bars, and a few health checks.

This checklist follows the room-by-room approach of the CDC's STEADI home safety checklist. Walk through the home with it, check items off (progress saves in this browser), or download the PDF to share with family.

General safety information, not medical advice. Talk to your doctor about your fall risk.
